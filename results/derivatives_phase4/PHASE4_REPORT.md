**MIXED — DERIVATIVES SIGNAL EXISTS BUT ECONOMIC EDGE IS NOT YET ROBUST.**

Data availability: funding is reproducibly available for 13/13 assets; OI, liquidations and basis fail the historical-data feasibility screen. Predictive evidence is recorded in the univariate and IC files. Economic test used one predeclared development-calibrated crowding filter; it was not tuned on 2026 or September. Its Jan–Aug OOS net return was 28.49%, versus baseline 19.72%; mean 14d incremental return 0.40%.

No candidate reached the required economic validation threshold, so Sep 1–20 was **not evaluated as a strategy holdout**. No ML or ablation was justified. Further research should obtain reliable historical OI/liquidation/basis data rather than add OHLCV transformations.
